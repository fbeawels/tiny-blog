# TinyBlog Interface

## Summary
This image depicts the user interface of a blog platform called "TinyBlog." The layout is clean and minimalistic, featuring a sidebar for navigation and a main content area for displaying blog posts. The design emphasizes simplicity and ease of use.

## Detailed Description

### Overall Layout and Structure
- The interface is divided into two main sections: a left sidebar and a main content area.
- The layout follows a standard blog design with a clear hierarchy of information.

#### Sidebar
- **Position**: Located on the left side of the interface.
- **Width**: Occupies approximately 20% of the screen width.
- **Components**:

    - **TinyBlog**: A category specific to the TinyBlog platform.
    - **Unclassified**: A category for posts that do not fit into other categories.

- **Position**: Located on the right side of the interface.
- **Width**: Occupies approximately 80% of the screen width.
- **Components**:
  - **Header**: Displays the title of the current post or section.
    - **Title**: "Welcome in TinyBlog"
    - **Date**: "26 April 2025"
  - **Content**: The main body of the text.
    - **Text**: "TinyBlog is a small blog engine made with Pharo."

### Visual Elements
- **Colors**:
  - **Background**: Light gray.
  - **Sidebar**: White with a light gray border.
  - **Selected Category**: Blue background with white text.
  - **Text**: Black.
- **Styles**:
  - **Fonts**: Clean, sans-serif fonts.
  - **Icons**: Minimal use of icons; primarily text-based navigation.
- **Spacing**: Adequate padding and margins ensure a clean and uncluttered look.

### Text Content and Formatting
- **Header Text**: Bold and larger font size to distinguish it from the body text.
- **Body Text**: Standard font size and weight, easy to read.
- **Date**: Smaller font size, positioned below the header title.

### Distinctive Features or Patterns
- **Minimalistic Design**: The interface is free of unnecessary elements, focusing on the essentials.
- **Clear Navigation**: The sidebar provides easy access to different categories, making it simple for users to find relevant content.
- **Highlighting**: The selected category in the sidebar is highlighted with a blue background, making it easy to see which category is currently active.

## Notable Observations
- The design is straightforward and functional, prioritizing usability over aesthetic complexity.
- The use of a light color scheme creates a bright and inviting atmosphere, which is conducive to reading.
- The layout is responsive, ensuring that it can adapt to different screen sizes and devices.
